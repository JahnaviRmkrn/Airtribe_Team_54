One-shot real room-response delay

Only Alice fixture organizer's next exact room GET is delayed, after the real route and SQL finish. Other roles, rooms, mutations and later GETs proceed normally. One pending/held response only. This is response delivery ordering, not simultaneous SQL execution.

For stale-close proof, wait for status to say held/open, close in the room tab, then release here before the room's 15-second GET timeout. Released old bytes must not reopen the room. Status reports elapsed hold time; an abort is timeout evidence, not stale-release evidence. The fixed 20-second mode exercises that actual 15-second timeout; next normal poll recovers. No mutations are delayed or retried. Reset/stop/client abort discard held bytes and timers.

Fixture lifecycle
