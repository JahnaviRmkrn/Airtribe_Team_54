Privacy policy

Airtribe Team 54 is a hackathon demo of a personal relationship manager and meetup planner.

What we store: when you sign in (email, or Google / Facebook / LinkedIn / Instagram), we receive your name, email address or username, and profile picture from the provider. We store that with the contacts, goals, meetups and room preferences you enter yourself.

What we don't do: we don't sell or share your data, or post to your social accounts. muop sends a message only if you have chosen to link WhatsApp or SMS (below) and tap Send. Guests in a meetup room see only the name and preferences you enter in that room.

Optional linked messaging: if you link your WhatsApp (an unofficial linked-device connection; WhatsApp may restrict accounts that use it) we store an encrypted session and your synced contact list on our server until you unlink, which logs the device out and deletes both. If you link SMS through the SMS Gateway for Android app we store its username and password encrypted until you unlink. We never log message text or phone numbers.

Deleting your data: delete contacts and meetups in the app at any time. To remove your account and all its data, ask the team (Jahnavi Ramakrishna or Arvind C R) and we'll delete it.

This demo runs on a team member's machine and may be reset or shut down without notice.
