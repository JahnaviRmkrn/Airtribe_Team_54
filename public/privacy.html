Privacy policy

Airtribe Team 54 is a hackathon demo of a personal relationship manager and meetup planner.

What we store: when you sign in (email, or Google / Facebook / LinkedIn / Instagram), we receive your name, email address or username, and profile picture from the provider. We store that with the contacts, goals, meetups and room preferences you enter yourself.

What we don't do: we don't sell or share your data, send messages on your behalf, or post to your social accounts. Guests in a meetup room see only the name and preferences you enter in that room.

Deleting your data: delete contacts and meetups in the app at any time. To remove your account and all its data, ask the team (Jahnavi Ramakrishna or Arvind C R) and we'll delete it.

This demo runs on a team member's machine and may be reset or shut down without notice.
